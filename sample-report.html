Measured on the live site

  • Fix first

    4 links have no readable text, so screen readers announce them as blank.

  • Fix first

    A loading indicator was still showing after about 4 seconds on the event page, with no content arriving.

  • Improve

    10 tap targets are under the recommended 44px on a phone, and 1 is below the 24px minimum.

  • Improve

    2 scripts block the page from showing until they download.

Three ways forward

Hours of work

Fix the measured problems

  • Give the 4 blank links readable labels
  • Stop the event page spinner
  • Let the page show before scripts load
Days of work

Make gift cards and events feel solid

  • Remember the chosen gift card amount
  • Enlarge small tap targets
  • A real loading state with a fallback
Weeks of work

Build it so problems don’t come back

  • Shared state across pages
  • One rule for how scripts load
  • Accessible links and buttons built in
