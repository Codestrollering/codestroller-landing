Larkspur & Fern is a fictional neighbourhood florist we built to show Codestroller at work, and Bloom Street Flowers is its fictional competitor. Everything below comes from a real Codestroll of those two sites: the grades, the customers’ own words, the measurements and the wireframes.

Verdict Not ready yet

Worth keeping

  • The founding story and “Since 2014”, which builds local trust.
  • A specific address with a “come say hello” tone.
  • Weddings told as a story, “elopements to grand celebrations”.

Worth borrowing from Bloom Street

  • Today’s hours on the home page.
  • The same-day cutoff, fee and delivery area, stated up front.
  • A starting wedding price and a bookable consultation.
Part 2

A graded report,one visit at a time

Each customer walked the site in a real browser with one goal. Their grades say how well the site got them there. The quotes are their own words at the end of the visit.

A
MayaNew neighbourhood residentLearn about the shop’s story and location before deciding to visit in person.3 steps
“This was easy. I went to the About page and immediately got the shop’s story plus their address. Then I checked Contact and found hours and phone number right away. I feel ready to visit in person.”

What worked

  • About page gave the story and address in one click.
  • Hours and phone were on the Contact page.

Where it fell short

  • Nothing stopped her. Bloom Street still puts hours on its home page, which saves a visit like Maya’s two clicks.
D
DerekLast-minute anniversary buyerFind a bouquet he can have delivered today, and check same-day delivery is possible.10 steps
“I found the Peony Garden bouquet and confirmed same-day delivery works if I order before 1pm, which was great. But when I got to checkout it showed a totally different bouquet than what I picked, and the cart icon still said zero items. I’m now not confident my wife is actually getting what I ordered.”

What worked

  • He found a bouquet he liked and set today’s date.
  • Same-day delivery was available before 1pm.

Where it failed

  • The cutoff was only in a collapsed FAQ on a separate page.
  • Checkout showed the Seasonal Market Bouquet, not the Peony Garden he added.
  • The cart counter still read (0).

The fix: make the cart show the bouquet that was chosen and update its counter, then put the 1pm same-day cutoff on the bouquet pages.

★ Most valuable
D
ElenaCouple planning a weddingFind out roughly what wedding flowers would cost and how to start working with the shop.10 steps
“I went looking for even a ballpark idea of what wedding flowers would cost, but the Weddings & Events page just told me to email them, with no price ranges, packages or past wedding examples to compare. I ended up filling out the contact form instead, but I still have no idea what this will cost me or whether anyone will respond quickly.”

Why she matters most

  • A wedding is the biggest order a florist takes, and a happy couple brings referrals for years.

Where it failed

  • No pricing, ranges or examples anywhere on the site.
  • The only next step was a general contact form, with no reply time.

The fix: give a starting price for wedding flowers, and swap “email us” for a wedding inquiry form that says when you’ll reply.

  2. Stuck

  3. Finished

Why it matters now

  • Phone and browser assistants are starting to shop and book for people. When one gets stuck, it just tries the next shop, and nobody tells you why.

Where it failed

  • A link that leads to a missing page, and a cart that holds the wrong bouquet.
  • The same-day cutoff hidden in collapsed text, which agents can’t open.
  • Six icon buttons and links with no name, and no structured data for the shop’s details or prices.

The fix: fix the “Shop now” link and carry the chosen bouquet into the cart, put the 1pm cutoff where it’s visible, label the icon buttons, and publish the shop’s details and prices as structured data.

Measured on every page

Checked by instruments, not opinions, so each one can be confirmed and re-tested.

  • S1Text that’s too faint to read comfortably: 5 elements fail the colour contrast minimum.Serious
  • S23 links with no text, such as the social icons, so screen readers announce them as blank.Serious
  • S31 tap target smaller than 24 × 24 px on a phone, below the WCAG 2.2 minimum.Friction
  • S43 tap targets under the recommended 44 × 44 px, fiddly with a thumb.Friction
  • S52 scripts in the page head load before anything shows, delaying every page.Friction
  • S63 links point to “#” and go nowhere.Friction

Three ways forward

Hours

Quick wins

  • Darken the 5 faint text elements.
  • Label the 3 icon links.
  • Enlarge the small tap targets.
  • Point the 3 “#” links somewhere real.
Days

Experience upgrade

  • Fix the cart so it holds the chosen bouquet and counts it.
  • Show the same-day cutoff where people choose.
  • Publish a starting wedding price.
  • Make more bouquets orderable online.
Weeks

Transformation

  • Rebuild Home and Weddings & Events around the three customers (see the wireframes).
  • Load the 2 head scripts without blocking the page.
