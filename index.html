Questions ownersask before a stroll

Check my website
Does Codestroller change my website?

No. It visits your site the way a customer would, in an ordinary browser. Nothing is installed, logged into or edited.

Who are the three customers, and can I change them?

They’re proposed from what your own site says about your business, and one is marked as the customer worth most to you. Before the stroll starts you can rename them, rewrite what they’re after, or pick a different most valuable customer.

How are competitors chosen?

Codestroller suggests similar businesses and opens each site to confirm it’s real. Suggestions can miss small local shops, so you can remove any and add the ones you actually compete with.

Do I need a developer?

Not to run a stroll or read the results. To make the changes, hand the fix list and sketched pages to whoever looks after your site — they’re written for that.

Is the wireframe a finished website?

No — it’s a plan: which pages to change, what goes where, and which customer each part is for. It’s built only from facts on your site, and anything missing, like a price or an event time, is marked for you to fill in.

What is the AI agent check?

An AI agent walks your site the way a shopping or booking assistant would, with tasks like finding a price, sending an enquiry or checking out. You get its grade, the step where it got stuck, and fixes that help agents and people alike. It’s new and in preview: the sample report shows what it looks like.

How long does it take?

About 30 seconds to meet your customers, then 10 to 15 minutes for the stroll itself. You can close the page while it runs; your results wait under Past strolls.
