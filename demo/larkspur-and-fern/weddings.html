Weddings & Events

Your wedding day deserves flowers as unique as your love story. Our designers work closely with every couple to create bouquets, boutonnieres, ceremony arches and centrepieces that reflect your style, your season and your venue.

We also design for corporate events, launches, dinners and parties of every size.

Every wedding is different, so we don’t have set packages. To start a conversation, email us at hello@larkspurandfern.example with your date, venue, colours, guest count, budget and any inspiration you love, and one of our designers will be in touch.
